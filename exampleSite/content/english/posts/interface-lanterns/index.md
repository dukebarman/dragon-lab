Some devices explain themselves with screens. Others speak through heat, rhythm, dust, and tiny lamps mounted where only a patient observer will notice them.

Don Oren treats each signal as a lantern: not proof by itself, but a reliable marker for where to look next. A glowing diode might show a sleeping sensor, a stalled handshake, or simply a wire that wants a better label.

The method works best when paired with the [moonlit bench protocol]({{< ref "/posts/observatory-protocols/" >}}) and reviewed later through [archive rituals]({{< ref "/posts/archive-rituals/" >}}).
