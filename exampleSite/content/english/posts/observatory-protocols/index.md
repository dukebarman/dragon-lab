Don Oren begins every investigation by naming the parts that can be seen without opening the case: ports, labels, hinges, screws, and the small lights that blink when nobody is touching the controls.

The first map is intentionally plain:

```text
signal source
  -> adapter
  -> recorder
  -> notebook
```

That map points to the later note on [interface lanterns]({{< ref "/posts/interface-lanterns/" >}}) and the slower maintenance pattern in [archive rituals]({{< ref "/posts/archive-rituals/" >}}).
